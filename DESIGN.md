# xiaominART Canvas Design Contract

## Reference style

The live `www.xiaominart.com` direction is the visual source for this iteration:

- Warm paper canvas: `#f7f3ea`
- Ink-like dark text: `#25231f`
- Cinnabar action color: `#9b3d32`
- Soft paper panels: `#ede5d7`
- Fine beige rules: `#d9d0c1`
- Editorial serif headings with restrained sans-serif body copy
- Square corners, no generic card shadows, large whitespace and numbered editorial chapters
- Desktop rhythm: left copy / right visual; mobile rhythm: stacked content with full-width CTAs

## Product goal and audience

Build a trust-first art brand site around the path: choose a blessing -> share a story -> listen before writing -> receive a traceable original work -> optionally share an authorized story. Primary audience is Chinese-speaking people seeking a meaningful, non-mass-produced calligraphy gift or home object; secondary audience includes overseas Chinese readers and B2B buyers.

## Current improvements

- Kept the public anchors `#intentions`, `#seasons`, `#works` and `#custom` for compatibility with existing links.
- Replaced the earlier blue-accent treatment with the live warm paper / cinnabar direction.
- Added a responsive season tab interaction for spring, summer, autumn and winter.
- Added a mobile navigation button with keyboard Escape and link-close behavior.
- Added a persistent header CTA without introducing checkout, payment, inventory or order promises.
- Kept the concept visual explicitly labelled as non-production artwork.

## Content boundary

The current visual asset is a locally authored concept SVG. It is not a production photo and must not be presented as an available physical work. Real calligraphy scans, product photography, process video, materials, dimensions, pricing and delivery terms require owner confirmation before publication.
